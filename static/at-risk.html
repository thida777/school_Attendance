How the risk level is decided

The highest matching level is used. Trend = last 4 weeks compared with the 4 weeks before.

LevelRule (any one is enough)
CriticalRate below 70%, or absent 5+ school days in a row
HighRate 70–79%, or absent 3–4 days in a row, or trend down 10+ points
MediumRate 80–89%, or trend down 5–9 points, or 3+ absences with no reason this month
LowRate 90% or higher, and the trend is stable or improving

A risk level is an early warning, not a prediction. People decide what to do.
