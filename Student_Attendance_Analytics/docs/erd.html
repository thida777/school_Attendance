2. Data dictionary: school data

Users, the school structure, students and their parents
Page 2 of 3 · Student Attendance Analytics System

Plan v2.0 rules built into the database: only Admin and Teacher log in · parents are contacts that receive Telegram or SMS messages · attendance is only Present or Absent, with an optional absence reason.

users
ColumnTypeDescription
idbigintPrimary key
namevarcharName in Latin letters
name_kmvarcharName in Khmer
emailvarcharUnique, optional
phonevarcharUnique, optional; can be used to log in
photo_pathvarcharOptional profile photo; empty = show the initials
telegram_chat_idvarcharOptional; the school bot sends the "Forgot password?" code here
passwordvarcharHashed (bcrypt), never plain text
roleenumadmin or teacher
localevarcharPreferred language: en or km
last_login_attimestampOptional; last login (web or app)
email_verified_attimestampLaravel default, optional
remember_tokenvarcharFor "Remember me"

Only school staff have accounts. The role decides which screens a user can open.

Unique: (academic_year_id, name). Named school_classes because "class" is a reserved word in PHP.

3. Data dictionary: attendance, analytics and parent contact

Lessons, records, the audit log, follow-ups, parent messages and early warnings

attendance_sessions
ColumnTypeDescription
idbigintPrimary key
school_class_idbigintFK → school_classes.id
subject_idbigintFK → subjects.id
teacher_idbigintFK → users.id (the teacher of the lesson)
recorded_bybigintFK → users.id, optional (who pressed Submit)
datedateDay of the lesson
periodtinyintLesson period, 1 to 8

One lesson where attendance was taken. Unique: (school_class_id, date, period), so the same lesson cannot be recorded twice.

attendance_records
ColumnTypeDescription
idbigintPrimary key
attendance_session_idbigintFK → attendance_sessions.id
student_idbigintFK → students.id
statusenumpresent or absent, nothing else
absence_reasonenumOptional: not_given, sick, family_matter, school_activity, other
notevarcharOptional

One row per student per lesson. Unique: (attendance_session_id, student_id). All analytics come from this table. Rate = present ÷ all records.

attendance_corrections
ColumnTypeDescription
idbigintPrimary key
attendance_record_idbigintFK → attendance_records.id
changed_bybigintFK → users.id (who made the change)
old_statusenumpresent or absent, before
new_statusenumpresent or absent, after
statusenumapplied, pending (teacher's request after 24 h), approved, rejected
reasonvarcharWhy it was changed; required after 24 hours
decided_bybigintFK → users.id, optional (admin who decided)
decided_attimestampOptional; when it was decided
decision_notevarcharOptional; the admin's note
created_attimestampWhen the change was made

Audit log. Within 24 hours a teacher's change is applied at once; after 24 hours it becomes a request the admin approves or rejects. Records are never changed silently.

interventions
ColumnTypeDescription
idbigintPrimary key
student_idbigintFK → students.id
created_bybigintFK → users.id
actionvarcharphone_call, telegram, sms, meeting, home_visit, talk, counselling, other
notestextOptional
action_datedateWhen the action was taken
follow_up_datedateWhen to check again
rate_before / rate_afterdecimalRate in the 10 school days before the action, and in the 10 after (worked out by the system)
outcometextOptional; what happened, written when the follow-up is closed
statusenumopen or closed
closed_attimestampWhen it was closed

Follow-up actions for at-risk students. rate_before and rate_after show whether the action helped.

parent_messages
ColumnTypeDescription
idbigintPrimary key
guardian_idbigintFK → guardians.id
student_idbigintFK → students.id
sent_bybigintFK → users.id; empty = sent by the system
typevarcharabsence_alert (automatic: 3 school days absent in a row) or manual (written by staff)
channelenumtelegram or sms
bodytextMessage text, Khmer then English
statusenumpending, sent, failed
simulatedbooleanYes = saved only (demo mode or SMS), not really delivered
errorvarcharWhy sending failed
replyvarchar(20)The parent's answer with a button in Telegram: sick, family_matter (also written as the absence reason) or call_me (opens a phone-call follow-up); empty = no answer yet. One answer per message
replied_attimestampWhen the parent answered
sent_attimestampWhen it was delivered

Every message to a parent, so the school can prove what was sent and when.

alerts
ColumnTypeDescription
idbigintPrimary key
teacher_idbigintFK → users.id (who should see it)
typeenumabsent_streak, risk_up, not_recorded
student_idbigintFK → students.id, for student alerts
timetable_slot_idbigintFK → timetable_slots.id, for "not recorded"
datedateDay of the alert
messagevarchare.g. Absent 3 school days in a row
handled_attimestampWhen the teacher handled it
handled_bybigintFK → users.id

Early warnings. Risk levels themselves are calculated, not stored: Critical (<70% or 5+ days in a row), High (70–79%, 3–4 days or down 10+ points), Medium (80–89% or down 5–9), Low.

settings
ColumnTypeDescription
keyvarcharPrimary key, e.g. school_name_en, school_name_km
valuetextThe value set by the admin on the School settings page

School-wide settings. The school name appears on the website, in printed reports, in Telegram messages and in the Android app (through GET /api/v1/school).
