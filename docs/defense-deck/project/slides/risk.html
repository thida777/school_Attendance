Trend = the last 4 weeks compared with the 4 weeks before. A risk level is an early warning, not a prediction: people decide what to do.

This is the heart of the analytics. I chose simple rules instead of a black-box model, because a teacher must be able to check why a student is flagged. The highest matching level is used. Critical means under 70 percent, or five school days absent in a row. High includes a drop of ten points compared with the four weeks before. Medium also catches absences with no reason. Each level comes with a suggested action. And I am careful with the wording: it is an early warning, not a prediction; people decide.
