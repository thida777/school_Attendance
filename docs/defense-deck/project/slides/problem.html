The problem

Paper attendance tells the school too late

Paper registers

Counted by hand, easy to lose, and hard to search. Nobody sees a pattern across weeks or classes.

Late warning

A student can miss many lessons before a teacher or the school office notices that something is wrong.

Parents find out late

There is no quick, recorded way to tell a parent that their child is often absent.

Question of this thesis: how can a high school see absence early, act on it, and know whether the action helped?

In many high schools attendance is still on paper. The register exists, but nobody can see patterns in it: which student is slipping, which class or day is worst. By the time someone notices, the student may already be far behind. And parents are informed late, often without any record. So my question was: how can a school see absence early, act on it, and measure whether the action helped?
