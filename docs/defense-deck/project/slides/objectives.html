Objectives

What the system had to do

1

Fast attendance

Every lesson, in about a minute, on the website or the Android app.

2

Early warning

Find students at risk with clear rules that a teacher can check.

3

Act and measure

Record follow-ups, message parents, and compare attendance before and after.

4

Made for Cambodia

Khmer and English everywhere, Telegram for parents, works on any phone.

From that problem I set four objectives. One: taking attendance must be fast, about a minute per lesson, on the website or the app. Two: the system must warn early, with rules simple enough that a teacher can check them by hand. Three: the school must be able to act, record follow-ups, message parents, and measure whether attendance changed. Four: it must fit a Cambodian school: Khmer and English everywhere, and Telegram, which parents already use.
