Interventions

Detect → Act → Measure → Evaluate

Act

Record the follow-up: phone call, meeting, home visit, message…

Measure

The system compares the rate in the 10 school days before and after.

Evaluate

Improved: +5 points or more. Declined: −5 or less. Demo data: 2 of 3 improved, 1 declined.

Finding a student is only half the job, so the system closes the loop. From the At-Risk list the admin presses Follow up and records what the school did. On the follow-up day, one button measures the result: the system compares the rate in the ten school days before and after the action, with no typing of numbers. Five points or more is Improved, minus five or less is Declined. In the demo data, two of three closed follow-ups improved and one declined, and I think it is important that the system shows failures too. It shows a change, not proof that the action caused it.
