Taking attendance

One lesson in about a minute

1

Today’s lessons

From the timetable: the next class is ready.

2

Mark

Everyone starts present; tap only the absent.

3

Review with reasons

Sick, family matter, school activity or other.

4

Submit

Changes allowed for 24 hours; after that the admin approves a request, and every change is logged.

This is the teacher's daily job. The app opens on today's lessons from the timetable, with the next class ready. Everyone starts as present, so the teacher only taps the absent students. Then they review, add a reason such as sick or family matter, and submit. For 24 hours the teacher can still change it; after that, a correction request goes to the admin, and every change is written to the audit log. The screenshot is the real app on the online server.
