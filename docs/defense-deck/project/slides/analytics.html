Analytics

From records to decisions

Dashboard

Rate this month, risk counts and who needs attention first.

Insights

Plain-language findings, a day × period heat map, and drill-down from grade to class.

At-Risk list

Priority order with the reason, and an Excel export.

The rules feed three views. The admin dashboard shows the rate this month, how many students are at High or Critical risk, and who to contact first. The Insights page writes findings in plain language, for example "Grade 11 shows the largest decline, mostly in 11B", and has a heat map of day and period, so the school can see that Monday period 1 is the weakest slot. The At-Risk list puts students in priority order with the reason, and it can be exported to Excel. Teachers get the same, limited to their own classes.
