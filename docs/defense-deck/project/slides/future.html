Limitations and future work

What is still missing, and what comes next

Limitations

Tested on generated data, not yet in a real school.

SMS is saved as a demo: the school has no SMS provider yet.

Free hosting: 1 GB disk and a shared processor.

Future work

A pilot term in a real school, with teachers’ feedback.

Parents reply to the bot; an SMS gateway for parents without Telegram.

After a full year of data: test a prediction model against the simple rules.

I want to be honest about the limits. Everything was tested on generated data; the next step is a pilot term in a real school, with feedback from real teachers. SMS is only saved as a demo, because that needs a paid provider. And the free hosting has a small disk and a shared processor, which is fine for a school of this size but not for many schools. For future work: let parents reply to the bot, add an SMS gateway, and, after a full year of real data, test whether a prediction model does better than the simple rules, which today have the advantage that anyone can check them.
