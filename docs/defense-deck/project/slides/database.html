The database has 15 project tables in three groups. Nine hold the school itself: users, classes, subjects, students, parents and the timetable. Three hold attendance: a session is one lesson, a record is one student in that lesson, and corrections is the audit log. Three support analytics and contact: follow-ups, every message sent to parents, and alerts. Three rules keep the data clean: one lesson per class, period and day; a status is only present or absent, with a reason; and every change is logged instead of deleted. The full ERD and data dictionary are in my report.
